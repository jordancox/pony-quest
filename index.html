Loose Shetland closes M25

“He seemed excited rather than distressed”Paul Anthem · Thames Valley Police

A Shetland wandered onto the M25 near the M4 interchange, travelling north along the southbound carriageway. Four officers and members of the public caught it; a nearby field of Shetlands was thought to be its home.
